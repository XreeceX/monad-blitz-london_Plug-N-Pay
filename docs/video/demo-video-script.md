# Plug-N-Pay — 35-Second Demo Video Script

Promo video for **Plug-N-Pay**: plug your EV in, and payment streams automatically from the car's wallet to the charger — per second, on Monad. No phone, no card, no app, no invoice. Unplug and the payment simply stops.

**Target length:** 30–40 seconds (script is timed to ~35s)
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (make 9:16 vertical cut by reframing on the car + dashboard)
**Style:** cinematic, warm late-afternoon light, clean modern EV + charging station, subtle tech-UI overlays. No dialogue — VO + music + on-screen text.
**Music:** minimal electronic pulse; heartbeat-like tick that syncs with the per-second payment counter; resolves warm and upbeat at the end.

---

## One-paragraph brief (paste into any video agent as global context)

> A driver in a modern electric car notices the battery is nearly empty and pulls into a sleek EV charging station at golden hour. Reaching to pay, he realises he has no phone, no wallet, no card — a beat of panic. Then he smiles: his car pays for itself. He plugs the charging cable in; the dashboard lights up with "Plug-N-Pay · Connected — Streaming payment on Monad," and a balance ticks down in tiny per-second increments (MON) exactly matching the kWh flowing in. Close-ups alternate between electrons-style light pulsing along the cable and the payment ticker pulsing in sync — energy and money moving at the same rhythm. Charged enough, he unplugs; the ticker instantly stops — no receipt, no checkout, nothing owed. He drives off into the evening, relaxed. End card: "Plug-N-Pay — Energy in. Payment out. Same second. Built on Monad."

## Scene-by-scene

### Scene 1 — "Low battery" (0:00–0:06)

- **Shot:** Interior, over-shoulder of a man driving a modern EV at golden hour. Dashboard shows battery icon at 4%, soft warning chime. He glances down, mild concern.
- **On-screen text:** none
- **VO:** "Running on empty…"
- **Video-gen prompt:**
  > Cinematic interior shot of a man in his 30s driving a modern electric car at golden hour, dashboard display glowing with a low battery warning at 4%, he glances at it with mild concern, shallow depth of field, warm sunlight through windshield, photorealistic, 4k.

### Scene 2 — Pull into the station (0:06–0:11)

- **Shot:** Exterior wide. The EV glides into a clean, futuristic charging station (2–3 charging pillars, minimal design). He steps out.
- **On-screen text:** none
- **VO:** "…and no way to pay."
- **Video-gen prompt:**
  > Wide cinematic exterior shot, a sleek electric car pulls smoothly into a minimalist futuristic EV charging station at sunset, two white charging pillars with soft LED accents, the driver steps out, warm golden light, long shadows, photorealistic, 4k.

### Scene 3 — The panic beat (0:11–0:16)

- **Shot:** Medium close-up. He pats his pockets — no phone, no wallet, no card. Quick 3-cut rhythm: empty pocket / empty cup holder through car window / his face, worried. Then his expression flips — a small knowing smile.
- **On-screen text (small, lower third):** `No phone. No card. No cash.`
- **VO:** "No phone. No card. No cash. …No problem."
- **Video-gen prompt:**
  > Medium close-up of a man at an EV charging station patting his jacket and jeans pockets finding nothing, glancing into the car for his phone, brief worry on his face turning into a confident smile, golden hour rim lighting, cinematic, photorealistic, 4k.

### Scene 4 — The plug-in moment (hero shot) (0:16–0:22)

- **Shot:** Close-up, slow motion. The charging connector clicks into the car's port. A soft ring of light pulses around the port. Cut to dashboard: UI panel animates in — `Plug-N-Pay · Connected` → `Streaming payment on Monad`.
- **On-screen text (dashboard UI):**
  - `Plug-N-Pay · Connected`
  - `Rate: 0.0021 MON / sec · 47 kW`
  - a per-second ticking balance: `-0.0021 … -0.0042 … -0.0063 MON`
- **VO:** "His car has its own wallet. Plug in — and payment streams, per second, matched to every electron."
- **Video-gen prompt:**
  > Slow-motion macro shot of an EV charging connector clicking into a glowing charge port, a soft ring of cyan light pulses around the port on connection, cut to a car dashboard screen where a clean payment interface appears reading "Plug-N-Pay Connected — streaming payment", numbers ticking down in tiny increments every second, futuristic UI, cinematic lighting, photorealistic, 4k.

### Scene 5 — Energy and money in sync (0:22–0:28)

- **Shot:** Split rhythm montage: (a) stylised light pulses travelling along the charging cable, (b) the dashboard ticker incrementing in the same rhythm, (c) a subtle blockchain-style confirmation pulse (small green check per tick). Heartbeat music synced to ticks.
- **On-screen text (lower third):** `Every second settled on Monad · No invoice. No app. No subscription.`
- **VO:** "Settled live on Monad — the only chain fast enough and cheap enough to pay by the second."
- **Video-gen prompt:**
  > Rhythmic montage: glowing energy pulses travel along an EV charging cable in the dusk, intercut with a dashboard payment counter ticking down in small increments in perfect sync, tiny green confirmation checks appearing each second, elegant fintech UI aesthetic, cinematic, high contrast, 4k.

### Scene 6 — Unplug, done (0:28–0:33)

- **Shot:** He unplugs the connector. The dashboard ticker freezes instantly: `Session complete · 4.83 MON · Fully settled`. No checkout screen, no card terminal. He gives the charger a small tap like saying thanks.
- **On-screen text (dashboard):** `Session complete — already paid.`
- **VO:** "Unplug — and it's already paid. Nothing owed, nothing to sign."
- **Video-gen prompt:**
  > A man unplugs an EV charging connector at dusk, the car dashboard payment counter instantly freezes and displays "Session complete — fully settled", he taps the charging pillar casually and smiles, no payment terminal in sight, warm evening light, cinematic, photorealistic, 4k.

### Scene 7 — Drive off + end card (0:33–0:38)

- **Shot:** The EV pulls away into the evening, tail lights glowing. Cut to end card on dark background with logo.
- **End card text:**
  - `PLUG-N-PAY`
  - `Energy in. Payment out. Same second.`
  - `Built on Monad · Monad Blitz London 2026`
- **VO:** "Plug-N-Pay. Energy in, payment out — same second. Built on Monad."
- **Video-gen prompt:**
  > Cinematic wide shot of an electric car driving away from a futuristic charging station into a purple-orange dusk, tail lights streaking softly, then cut to a minimal dark end card with bold white text "PLUG-N-PAY — Energy in. Payment out. Same second. Built on Monad", subtle glowing accent line, 4k.

## Notes for specific tools

- **Google Gemini / Veo:** Feed the one-paragraph brief as context, then generate scene by scene using each scene's prompt; ask for 5–7s clips per scene at 16:9, consistent character ("man in his 30s, olive jacket, modern white EV") in every prompt to keep continuity.
- **Higgsfield / Kling / Runway / Pika:** Use the per-scene prompts directly; add `--camera slow push-in` (Scene 4) and `--camera static` (end card). Generate Scene 4 twice — it's the hero shot.
- **Character consistency tip:** Add to every prompt: *"same man: early 30s, short dark hair, olive green jacket; same car: white modern electric sedan."*
- **UI overlays:** If the generator struggles with legible dashboard text, render Scene 4/5/6 UI as a separate screen-capture overlay (simple HTML mock of the Plug-N-Pay ticker) and composite in an editor — real, legible numbers beat AI-hallucinated text.
- **Vertical cut:** Reframe Scenes 1, 3, 4, 6 for 9:16; drop Scene 2 if you need to hit 30s.
