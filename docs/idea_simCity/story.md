# The Story

Maya is mayor for an afternoon.

She opens a map that looks like a toy city — colored tiles, tiny dots moving between housing blocks and factory yards. Under the map, a feed scrolls too fast to read every line:

`Agent 08 worked at Mill A · +0.004 MON`  
`Agent 23 bought food · District C`  
`Agent 11 moved → (4,6)`  

A counter in the corner says **City GDP** and another says **TPS**. They are the same story told two ways: how busy the city is, and how busy Monad is.

Maya slides the tax rate up. Nothing explodes. Then, quietly, dots start leaving the industrial edge for cheaper districts. A gossip line appears: *Agent 17 quit after tax hike.* The treasury ticks up. Wages still settle. The city does not pause for a loading screen — because there is no server pretending to be a world. The ledger *is* the world.

She hits **Blackout**.

Energy stalls. Prices jump. Agents scramble — some overpay, some stop working, a few try to game housing. The settlement feed spikes. For thirty seconds the map looks like a panic, and every panicky step is a transaction that already finalized.

Maya laughs. She did not build a game with crypto stickers on it. She spun up a place where policy has consequences at the speed of the chain — and the proof is right there in the pulse of the city.
