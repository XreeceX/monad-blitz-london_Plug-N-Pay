# The Story

Sam is sitting in row 4. Laptop half-closed. It’s 19:40, this is the 22nd pitch of the night, and Sam is tired.

**0:00 — “Scan this.”**  
A QR fills the projector. Sam scans it with the phone camera, the way anyone scans a menu. A web page opens. No app store, no install, no signup, no seed phrase, no “connect wallet” popup.

> You’ve been hired.  
> Balance: 0.000 MON  
> Your worker ID: 0x7a4f…c2e1  
> Waiting for your first task…

A wallet was generated in the browser and saved to local storage. Sam didn’t do anything and doesn’t need to understand it.

**0:25 — The job.**  
Two logos. Five seconds. Sam taps B. On the projector, A vs B bars move as the room votes. Workers online climbs.

**0:50 — Payment lands.**  
The presenter says “check your phone.” Sam’s screen changes on its own.

> +0.02 MON  
> Balance: 0.020 MON  
> tx 0x9c1b… ↗ view on monadscan  
> Paid 0.4s ago

The number went up. Sam didn’t ask for it, didn’t sign anything, didn’t pay gas. The projector’s payroll ticker scrolls: workers paid, total wages, live hashes. Sam taps the Monadscan link out of reflex — because devs always check — and there it is: a real transfer to Sam’s own address, from an agent’s wallet.

**1:10 — Second job.**  
A counting question. The whole room lifts its head at once. Laugh. Tap. Pay again.

**1:35 — The reveal.**  
The winning logo. Chosen by the room. Commissioned, graded, and paid for by an agent. Total wage bill on screen. No human touched the wallet.

**19:55 — Sam votes.**  
Twenty-one projects were slides and a screen recording. One of them paid Sam money and showed Sam the thing Sam helped make.
