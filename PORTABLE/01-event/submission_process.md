# Submission Process

## Steps to prepare your repo

1. Visit the `monad-blitz-london` repo (link here) and fork it.

!image.png

1. Give it your project name, a one-liner description, make sure you are forking `main` branch and click `Create Fork`

!image.png

1. In your fork you can make all the changes you want, add code of your project, create branches, add information to `README.md` , you can change anything and everything.
2. For next steps please head to Blitz.devnads.com

## Submitting project on Blitz Portal

1. On the event page, wait for submissions to open.

!image.png

1. When submissions open, the Submit Project tab will be clickable.
2. Fill in the details for your project.
    1. For Github URL enter your fork repo URL.
    2. If you don’t have a Demo URL, enter your GitHub URL again.

!image.png

1. When you are ready, submit your project by clicking the Submit Project button.
2. After your submission, you are allowed to change your submission UNTIL the voting period starts.
