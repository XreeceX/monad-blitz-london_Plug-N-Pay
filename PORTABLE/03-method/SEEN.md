## Verified dead — do not propose

- iOS Safari has no `torch` MediaTrack constraint → no camera-finger heart-rate.
- 167 phones sending direct txs → public Monad testnet RPC rate-limits. Dead by construction.
- Any live TPS counter fed by one batching relayer → theatre; caps Why-Monad at 3.
- DeviceMotionEvent needs HTTPS + per-device user gesture, partial iOS support →
  whole-room motion mechanics cap demo-survivability at 4.
